Privacy

What happens to what you paste

Last updated September 2026.

MogBot is an independent project, not a company. This page says plainly what happens to the job description, resume, and answers you give it — no legal filler.

What we collect

The job description and resume text you paste in, the answers you type during a practice session, and the questions, scores, and coaching report MogBot generates from them. There are no accounts, so we never ask for your name, email, or a login.

We also process your IP address briefly, to enforce rate limits and stop the service from being overloaded. It's held in memory only, never written to disk or linked to your session, and clears when the server restarts.

Where it goes

That text is sent to Anthropic's API (Claude) so the agents can generate questions and score your answers. Anthropic is the only third party involved. Their own privacy policy governs how they handle API requests on their end.

What gets stored, and for how long

Your session — the questions, your answers, scores, and the final report — is saved server-side so a page refresh or a brief outage doesn't lose your progress. Some generated results (question sets, evaluations, report sections) may also be cached and reused to speed up similar future requests.

Right now there's no automatic deletion — this is an early version, not a production data-retention pipeline. If you'd like a session removed, open an issue on the GitHub repository and we'll delete it.

What we don't do

We don't sell what you paste, run ads against it, or use it for anything beyond generating your practice session.

Your browser

The site also uses your browser's local storage to remember an in-progress draft so a refresh doesn't clear the form. That stays on your device — it's separate from anything stored on our server.

Changes

This is a small, evolving project. If how we handle data changes, this page will change with it.
