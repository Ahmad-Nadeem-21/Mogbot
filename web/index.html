How a session runs

  1. 01

    Add the role

    Paste the job description and your resume or LinkedIn text. No file uploads, no account.

  2. 02

    Answer as you would out loud

    Questions are generated from the specific overlap and gaps between the posting and your background.

  3. 03

    Get pushed on weak spots

    Vague, unsupported, or overconfident answers are challenged with a direct follow-up before you move on.

  4. 04

    Read the report

    A closing summary looks for patterns across all your answers, not just a per-question score.

Under the hood

MogBot isn't one prompt. Seven purpose-built agents split the work — researching the role, reading the resume, generating questions, evaluating answers, challenging weak ones, and writing the final report — and four review agents check the output before it reaches you. They share a vector memory so later agents can reuse what earlier ones already found.

Job Search Agent
Extracts role requirements, keywords, and interview focus areas from the posting.
Resume & Role Analyzer
Extracts your skills, evidence, and gaps against that role.
Question Generator
Builds and adapts questions from the research, your profile, and prior answers.
Interviewer
Decides conversation strategy — clarify, transition, or press further.
Evaluator
Scores each answer against a rubric and produces a reward signal.
Devil's Advocate
Challenges vague, inconsistent, or unsupported answers directly.
Career Coach
Synthesizes every answer into one evidence-grounded closing report.
